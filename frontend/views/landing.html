Host anything from home.
No port forwarding.

NatForge exposes a server running on your own machine - a game server, a website, an API, SSH - to the public internet, even behind NAT or CGNAT. No router config, no static IP, no VPS.

Your friends just open your-name.natforge.com.

Works behind CGNAT

Your machine makes one outbound connection - so it works behind any NAT or carrier-grade NAT, with nothing to open on your router.

Your own subdomain

Reach web apps over HTTP/HTTPS at name.natforge.com, or get a dedicated public port for raw-TCP servers like Minecraft.

Pick your region

Choose which regional server hosts your tunnel, so traffic takes the shortest path to your audience.

Encrypted & private

The control channel is TLS-encrypted with a pinned certificate, and traffic is relayed in memory only - nothing is written to disk.

How it works

From zero to a public link in three steps.

1

Reserve a tunnel

Sign in, pick a subdomain and a region, and add a route for each local port you want to expose.

2

Run the agent

Start the lightweight natforge agent on your machine with the printed command. It connects out and stays linked.

3

Share the link

Anyone who opens your subdomain (or the dedicated port) reaches your service - relayed through NatForge to your machine.
